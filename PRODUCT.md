# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Adults in Tampere looking for professional nail, lash, brow and foot-care services, primarily browsing on mobile before calling, emailing or requesting directions.

## Product Purpose

Present Jessica Mai Beauty as a professional salon, make services and current prices easy to understand, and turn visits into appointment enquiries.

## Brand Personality

Bold, polished, feminine. The first impression should feel dramatic and confident while the service information remains clear and practical.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic cream luxury templates, pastel wellness styling, decorative glass cards, excessive animation, copied reference-site identity, and any outdated business details.

## Design Principles

- Make the salon identity unmistakable in the first viewport.
- Let beauty photography carry the atmosphere.
- Use strong contrast and one warm accent for decisive calls to action.
- Keep pricing and contact information simpler than the decorative hero.
- Preserve usable mobile navigation and readable Finnish content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain strong text contrast, visible focus states, touch-friendly controls, reduced-motion support, semantic headings and descriptive alternative text.
